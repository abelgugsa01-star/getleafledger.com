Tobacco monthly reporting: choose the right workflow before importing

LeafLedger helps tobacco manufacturers and importers reconcile records and prepare supported report drafts on Windows. Start with your permit, reporting role and month; then choose the applicable workflow.

Example: one company, two roles

A business that both manufactures and imports should retain separate source support for each role. A shared accounting total is not enough to decide which form or column an entry belongs in. Mark each batch with its permit, activity, month and product class before reconciliation.

Prepare once, review every month

  1. Save the original exports and source documents.
  2. Map identifiers, dates and units; review conversions instead of assuming them.
  3. Run the applicable reconciliation and investigate missing or inconsistent facts.
  4. Compare the draft with source evidence, then have the responsible officer review, certify and submit through the appropriate process.

Keep deadlines separate

TTB lists the manufacturer report as due by the 20th of the following month and the importer report by the 15th. These are different deadlines; check current instructions and applicability for your operation. TTB operational report due dates.

Does LeafLedger submit my reports?

No. It prepares supported drafts and review evidence. Your business handles certification and submission.

Can missing records be treated as zero activity?

No. Confirm and document zero activity; an absent file is an unresolved input.
