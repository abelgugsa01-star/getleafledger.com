REFUNDS & CANCELLATION

Cancel any time. Here's how refunds work.

EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 15, 2026 · PROFITPIN LLC · SUPPORT@GETLEAFLEDGER.COM

The free month

IN PLAIN TERMSCancel any time in the first 30 days and you are never charged. Nothing to refund.

Every new subscription starts with 30 free days. A card is required to start, but no charge is made until the trial ends. Cancel from your billing page before the trial end date shown on your welcome page and Stripe receipt, and no charge will ever be made. We email a reminder before the trial ends.

Cancelling a paid subscription

IN PLAIN TERMSCancel whenever you like. It stops the next charge. You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for.

Open your billing page and choose Cancel — it takes effect immediately for future charges. Or email support@getleafledger.com from your subscription address and we'll cancel within one business day. Your access continues to the end of the current paid period; everything saved on your computer stays readable afterwards.

Refunds

IN PLAIN TERMSMonthly fees already charged aren't refunded for unused days — but if we got something wrong, tell us and we'll make it right.

Because the subscription is month to month with no minimum term and a free first month, we don't prorate or refund a monthly charge for unused days after you cancel.

We refund a charge in full if: you were charged after cancelling before the trial ended or before the renewal date; you were charged twice for the same period; or the App could not run on your Windows computer and we couldn't fix it within a reasonable time after you contacted support. Email support@getleafledger.com within 30 days of the charge. Refunds go back to the original card and usually appear within 5–10 business days. State consumer-protection rights that go beyond this policy still apply.

Disputing a charge with your bank

Please contact us first — most billing questions are fixed the same day. If you file a chargeback instead, the subscription is suspended while it's investigated, and if the charge was valid under these policies we'll provide the issuer with your checkout record.

After your subscription ends

The App stops preparing new reports. Drafts, evidence packages, and history already saved on your computer remain yours. Restart any time from getleafledger.com; a second free month isn't offered to the same business.
