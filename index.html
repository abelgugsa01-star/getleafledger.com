IS THIS FOR YOU?

Built for one job. Here is whether it's yours.

LeafLedger prepares monthly TTB and FDA tobacco reports from your own records, with the reconciliation that makes them defensible.

A good fit if

  • You hold a TTB permit as a domestic manufacturer of tobacco products (5210.5) or an importer (5220.6).
  • You file FDA 3852 monthly and want it to match TTB to the stick and the pound.
  • Your production, transfer and shipping data can be exported as CSV or Excel.
  • You want the draft, the evidence and the history kept together, month after month.

Not the right tool if

  • You are a retailer, distributor or wholesaler with no TTB reporting obligation.
  • You want a service that files for you — LeafLedger prepares; your officer signs and submits.
  • Your records exist only on paper.

What it is not

  • Not tax, legal or accounting advice; it never certifies or submits.
  • Not a cloud service: inventory, permit and tax data stay on your computer.
  • Not an excise tax calculator for other products (alcohol, firearms).

03 / A FEW ANSWERS

Know what
to expect.

Is there a walkthrough I can try first?＋

Yes. Your first month is a five-minute narrated tour of the Windows application with a downloadable practice workspace of fictional records. Nothing in it is a real business and nothing it produces can be filed.

Can I use LeafLedger today?＋

Yes. Start the free month on this page, download the Windows installer from the next screen and you are reconciling within minutes. Month to month, cancel any time.

Does LeafLedger submit my reports?＋

No. LeafLedger prepares and validates supported report drafts. The customer reviews the results and remains responsible for filing, signatures, and any required officer certification.

Is this a managed filing service?＋

No. LeafLedger is software customers operate themselves, using their recurring business records. It is not a consulting or outsourced filing service.

What happens when information is missing?＋

The engine flags missing or inconsistent information for review. It does not guess product classes, transaction types, tax amounts, permit identities, or unit conversions. An explicit zero-activity declaration is treated differently from missing data.

Does my reporting data leave my computer?＋

No. LeafLedger processes your records and prepares drafts locally; nothing is uploaded as part of preparing a month. The app makes one small call about every six hours — sending only your license key — to confirm your subscription is active. If you're offline, it keeps working for a week on the last confirmation, and your archived months stay readable regardless.

What do I need to start the free month?＋

A card, and about two minutes. Checkout is handled by Stripe; you're not charged for 30 days, and you can cancel before then from your billing page and never be charged. After checkout you get a license key and the download link.

Will my reporting data be uploaded here?＋

No. This website provides product information and a way to contact us. It does not accept transaction files or prepare filings. The product is designed to process reporting records locally on your computer.
