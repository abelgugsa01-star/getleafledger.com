How to read the result

  • Book closing is opening + manufactured + received − tax-paid removals − removals without tax − used, destroyed or lost.
  • Book to count compares that figure with your physical count. A difference is something to explain, not a number to adjust.
  • Tax tie-out multiplies tax-paid removals by the rate you entered and compares it with the tax on your return.
